Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chameleo GFX Studio ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects any information you provide while using our website, https://chameleogfxstudio.com/.

We are committed to safeguarding your privacy. If we request any personally identifiable information, rest assured that it will only be used in accordance with this policy. We may update this policy periodically, so please review this page to stay informed of any changes.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following details:

  • Your name and company name
  • Contact details, including email address and phone number
  • Demographic information such as location, preferences, and interests
  • Business-related information such as company profile, website, products, and services
  • Any other information relevant to customer inquiries, surveys, or promotional offers

How We Use Your Information

We collect this data to enhance our services and improve your experience. Specifically, we use it for:

  • Internal record-keeping and customer relationship management
  • Improving our products, web solutions, and creative design services
  • Sending updates, project proposals, promotions, or offers that may interest you
  • Market research and communication via email, phone, or digital messaging
  • Personalizing website content based on user interests

Data Security

We prioritize the security of your personal information and have implemented appropriate physical, electronic, and managerial measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small files placed on your device to enhance your browsing experience. We use traffic log cookies to analyze website usage and improve our services. These cookies only collect data for statistical analysis and help us deliver a faster, tailored experience.

You can choose to accept or decline cookies. Most browsers accept them by default, but you can adjust your settings to disable cookies if preferred. However, disabling cookies may impact certain website features.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites. Once you leave our site, we are not responsible for how those external websites handle your information. Please review the privacy policies of any third-party websites before submitting your data.

Managing Your Personal Information

We do not sell, distribute, or lease your personal information to third parties unless required by law or with your explicit consent. If you have opted to receive marketing communications, you can unsubscribe at any time.

If you believe any information we hold is incorrect or incomplete, or if you would like to request data deletion, please contact us at info@chameleogfxstudio.com and we will promptly handle your request.

For further inquiries, visit https://chameleogfxstudio.com/.
