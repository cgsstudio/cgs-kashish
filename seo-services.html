SEO · AEO · GEO

Search happens in three places now. We optimise all three.

A buyer might see a blue link, a featured snippet and an AI-generated summary in the same afternoon. Here is how the three disciplines differ — and why a 2019 SEO playbook no longer covers it.

Comparison of SEO, AEO and GEO
Dimension SEO — Search Engine AEO — Answer Engine GEO — Generative Engine
Where you appear Organic results on Google, Bing Featured snippets, People Also Ask, voice ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews
What wins Relevance, technical health, links Direct, well-structured answers Clear entities, original data, citations
Content format Comprehensive pages, clusters 40–60 word answers, lists, tables, FAQ Comparisons, definitions, quotable stats

AI search visibility

Your next customer is asking an AI, not scrolling a page

Generative engines do not rank ten links. They synthesise one answer from a handful of trusted sources. GEO is the work of making sure your brand is one of them — consistently, across every place your name, services and location are described.

  • Entity clarity. One canonical description of who you are, what you sell and where — repeated in the same words on your site, GBP, LinkedIn and directories.
  • Citable substance. Original benchmarks, pricing pages, comparison tables and definitions that a model can quote with attribution.
  • Machine-readable structure. FAQ, Service, Organization and LocalBusiness schema, plus an llms.txt file that tells AI crawlers what matters.
  • Third-party mentions. Reviews, listicles, Reddit and industry press — the sources models lean on when they name a shortlist.
